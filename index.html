Equations Used — ASHRAE Handbook Fundamentals

1. Jet Centerline Velocity Decay

Vx = V0 × K × (D0 / x)
Where:
Vx = Centerline velocity at distance x (m/s)
V0 = Initial fan outlet velocity (m/s)
K = Throw constant ≈ 6.2 for circular jets
D0 = Fan diameter (m)
x = Distance from fan (m)

Valid for x > 5×D0 (beyond potential core)

2. Radial Velocity Profile (Gaussian)

V(x,r) = Vx × exp(-0.693 × (r / r0.5)²)
Where:
r = Radial distance from jet centerline (m)
r0.5 = Jet half-width where V = 0.5×Vx
r0.5 ≈ 0.097x (jet spreads at ~11° half-angle)

Gaussian profile models turbulent mixing in jet

3. Draft Risk (DR) — ASHRAE Standard 55

DR = (34 - ta) × (v - 0.05)0.62 × (0.37×v×Tu + 3.14)
Where:
DR = Draft Risk (% dissatisfied), 0-100%
ta = Local air temperature (°C)
v = Local air velocity (m/s), min 0.05
Tu = Turbulence intensity (30-60% for fans)

DR predicts % of occupants dissatisfied due to draft

4. Turbulence Intensity in Fan Jets

Tu(x) = Tu0 + 0.05 × (x / D0)
Where:
Tu(x) = Local turbulence intensity
Tu0 = Initial turbulence ≈ 0.10 near fan
Turbulence increases with distance due to mixing

Typical range: 10-60% for electric fan jets
